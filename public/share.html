Toolkit · Shareables

Made to be clipped.

Some arguments spread as paragraphs; most spread as pictures. Here are the site's hardest facts as bold, sourced cards — sized for TikTok & Reels (9:16), Instagram & posts (1:1) and press & X (16:9). Download the PNG, copy the caption, and post. Every card carries its source and a link back to the receipts.

Free to share & republish — with or without credit, though a link to safetytheatre.theradicalparty.com helps people check the sources. Journalists welcome.

How to use these

  • TikTok / Reels / Stories — switch to Story 9:16, hit ↓ PNG, drop it in as a slide or background. Read the headline aloud; the stat does the rest.
  • Instagram / Threads / Bluesky — use Square 1:1. Copy caption pastes the fact, the source and a link.
  • Journalists / X / newsletters — Wide 16:9 drops straight into an article or a tweet. Every number traces to a government audit, budget paper or study on Sources.
  • No download button? Just screenshot the card — they're built to look right either way.

These cards summarise figures presented in full, with citations, across the site. Verify against the primary source before republishing — that's the whole point. Corrections: GitHub.
